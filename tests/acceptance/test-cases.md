# Acceptance test cases

Run the game at `http://localhost:8000/` in a current desktop browser and, for touch behavior, a mobile browser or device.
These checks map to the approved requirements and are intended to be performed on the integrated application.

| ID | Requirements | Check | Expected result |
|---|---|---|---|
| AT-01 | REQ-SYS-01/02/11 | Open the project root URL in a supported browser, including beneath a repository prefix, and resize to a narrow viewport. | Game setup is usable with working styles and modules, then the game renders as a responsive 2D platformer. |
| AT-31 | REQ-SYS-12 | During setup, play, and completion, choose a severity and use Bug report, Feature request, or General feedback; review and submit on GitHub after the template is deployed. | Category and severity are prefilled, GitHub identifies the signed-in submitter, and no photo, runner name, or score is automatically attached. |
| AT-32 | REQ-SYS-12/13 | Deploy a push to `develop`, note the feedback version, and open all three feedback categories at different severities. | The displayed and prefilled branch and full commit match the deployed source, independent of GitHub's default branch; unstamped local copies explicitly report Not injected. |
| AT-02 | REQ-SYS-03, REQ-LVL-01/03/04/16 | Upload images with sparse or busy visible contours and start a level. | Three playable choices retain useful photo edges and breathing room; density targets do not reject otherwise usable photos. |
| AT-02b | REQ-LVL-05 | Upload a uniform image without a visible edge. | The game rejects the image and clearly asks for another photo instead of creating an arbitrary floor. |
| AT-03 | REQ-LVL-04 | Use the camera capture control on a device with a camera; separately deny/cancel camera access and upload a file. | A captured photo can start a level; upload remains usable when capture is denied or unavailable. |
| AT-04 | REQ-SYS-04 | Start moving or charging, note the timer, then touch the exit flag. | Timer begins with player movement or charging, stops on contact with the flag, and the completion view shows the elapsed time. Merely crossing the flag's horizontal position at another height does not finish. |
| AT-05 | REQ-SYS-05 | Enter a valid name, then try digits, punctuation, and a 33-character value. | Only letters/spaces (maximum 32 characters) are accepted. |
| AT-06 | REQ-SYS-06 | Finish the same exact option twice, including a tied attempt, then finish outside its top ten. | Only the exact current run is clearly outlined and labeled; stable ties retain order; an unranked run appears below the ten with no place and is not persisted as an eleventh score. |
| AT-07 | REQ-MOV-01/04/10 | Use A/D, arrows, Space, and held Return on desktop; try W/up without drilling; use touch movement, jump, and drill controls. | Space alone controls keyboard jumping. W/up enters background climbing without drilling. Air movement remains at normal speed. |
| AT-08 | REQ-MOV-02/07 | Charge near each side of a terrain wall and at both vertical image borders, then release; repeat away from all surfaces. | Gravity continues during wall charging. Release within three cells launches up and away from terrain walls or the image side borders; distant releases never launch. |
| AT-09 | REQ-MOV-03/10 | Walk while drilling straight left and right across a supported floor, then hold drill through intact ground and open space; aim up while climbing and down while standing. | Horizontal drilling does not excavate the supporting floor below the player's feet. Active horizontal excavation travels at 20 cells/s, like climbing; open walking/drilling remains 80; gravity and jump charge are unchanged; directional tunnels remain permanent for the run. |
| AT-10 | REQ-CHR-01, REQ-LVL-02 | Inspect the character and generated overlay. | The character and retro pixel-art presentation are original and do not reproduce a named game's character or artwork. |
| AT-11 | REQ-LVL-06 | Inspect terrain tops, sides, undersides, interiors, and freshly drilled tunnels. | All exposed outlines are fully opaque; only interior filling is 80% opaque, keeping the photo visible through it. |
| AT-12 | REQ-MOV-05/06/07 | Hold jump for three seconds near a floor or wall, keep holding, then release; repeat with a quick tap and partial charge. | HUD and on-character bars fill; MAX READY and a gold character bar mark maximum; release launches with increasing height up to full-level height; no background or unattached air jumps occur. |
| AT-13 | REQ-SYS-08 | Drill terrain, move and charge, then choose Reset run. Repeat after completion. | Same photo and name, original undug terrain and spawn, zero timer and charge, no held controls, saved scores retained, and no score for an abandoned run. |
| AT-14 | REQ-MOV-08, REQ-SYS-09 | Drill downward through terrain to the image bottom, then walk and charge/release a jump. | Feet stop at the solid bottom; walking and jumping work without a respawn or timer/tunnel reset. |
| AT-15 | REQ-LVL-07 | Drill all support from beneath the flag. | The flag stays at its original position, even floating. No terrain is protected. |
| AT-16 | REQ-LVL-08/16 | Upload sparse, disconnected, or multi-height photo geometry. | Useful edges guide the level; longer bridges and supplemental horizontal platforms repair missing supports; the main route needs no drilling. |
| AT-17 | REQ-LVL-09/11 | Inspect a photo with long horizontal, diagonal, and vertical edges. | Diagonal contours remain connected, verticality prioritizes real vertical-ish edges, and no periodic rectangular cuts manufacture wall faces. |
| AT-18 | REQ-MOV-07 | Walk both ways on a slanted surface while charging; briefly lose contact, then return or release farther than three cells away. Press jump shortly before landing. | Small slopes retain support and charge. Charge survives 150 ms contact loss, but distant release cancels. A still-held press within 150 ms of contact starts charging; older presses do not. |
| AT-19 | REQ-CHR-02, REQ-MOV-05 | Inspect the runner and charge indicator while moving/charging and near the ceiling. | Runner is 6-by-9 cells; bar follows above it and remains inside the image. Normal speed and jump height are unchanged. |
| AT-20 | REQ-MOV-10 | Press Up, climb, release directions, then press Space or touch JUMP while holding Up; keep holding jump until landing, then release. | Jump press immediately drops without charging or launching; held Up does not reattach; holding through landing and releasing cannot launch; a fresh Up press reattaches. |
| AT-21 | REQ-CHR-03 | Open the game before selecting a photo and inspect the browser tab icon. | The icon depicts the original player character with its hat, teal outfit, orange scarf, and pixel-art silhouette. |
| AT-22 | REQ-LVL-10/11/12, REQ-CHR-04 | Generate suggestions from a route-capable mixed-edge photo and inspect all three thumbnails; choose each biome and climb/drill. | Local real-edge direction determines layout priority; biome scenery and headwear remain consistent, with no invented freestanding walls on single-orientation photos. |
| AT-23 | REQ-LVL-07/12 | Generate multiple batches, select an option, drill and reset, and finish/replay. | The complete two-pole checkered gate stays inside the image at safe support nearest the sampled edge; starts are distant; live geometry matches the preview; reset retains the option and scores are variant-specific. |
| AT-24 | REQ-LVL-10/13 | Inspect desert sunshine/rays, underwater bubbles/shafts, and jungle dappled light/motes; enable reduced motion; walk into and drill substantial scenery. | Effects remain behind gameplay and do not obscure the photo/finish; reduced motion freezes them; large trees, plants, cacti, coral, rocks, and bones use visible drillable collision silhouettes, with anchored grass/vine accents. |
| AT-25 | REQ-LVL-01/06/11/16 | Upload sparse photos and photos with enclosed or busy features; inspect choices and excavate. | Filling respects protected clearance; strongest useful edges and supplemental platforms repair cluttered or disconnected layouts; opacity is unchanged and density is not a rejection gate. |
| AT-26 | REQ-LVL-07 | Try to walk to the finish without jumping, climbing, or drilling, including along the picture bottom and down slopes. | Every offered option prevents a walk-only finish; the generator tries alternative placements and clearly reports unsuitable photos. |
| AT-27 | REQ-LVL-06/14 | Compare a busy original photo with all three previews and live play; drill new boundaries. | The photo remains recognizable but darkened/desaturated; dark noncolliding terrain borders improve separation, and opaque outlines/translucent interiors remain consistent. |
| AT-28 | REQ-LVL-12/15, REQ-SYS-08/10 | Inspect wasps, scarabs, and jellyfish; start the timer, drill, touch an enemy while holding controls, and manually reset. | Patrols stay in clear safe corridors and cannot get stuck; contact respawns only the character, clears held controls, keeps time/tunnels/enemy progress, and saves no score; reset restores all selected-option state. |
| AT-29 | REQ-LVL-01/08/09/11/16 | Follow the validated main route and separately try free-climbing, full-height-jump, and drilling shortcuts. | The contiguous photo-guided corridor has breathing room and a verified no-drill route; 4–6 sections and 20–40 seconds are preferences without idle padding; skilled shortcuts remain unrestricted. |
| AT-30 | REQ-LVL-01/03/05/07/08/09/11/12/16 | Upload the supplied portrait, sparse horizontal/vertical scenes, disconnected edges, and busy textures; separately upload a uniform image. | Edge-bearing photos offer three repaired, safe choices instead of numeric-target errors; supplemental platforms match preview/reset and can be drilled; the uniform source clearly asks for another photo. |
