Controls

A/D or ←/→ move. Press Space on a floor or wall to jump immediately; press again in the air to double jump.

W/↑ grips and climbs the background. Hold Enter to drill toward the pressed direction.

Touch controls are available below the game on phones and tablets.

Level elements

Photo edges form solid ground; striped sections are non-drillable, while tunnels can be drilled elsewhere.

Touch every glowing checkpoint to open the checkered exit gate.

Yellow lightning doubles movement speed for five seconds; spotted mushrooms subtract two seconds from the timer.

Flying patrols reset the run on contact, and moving platforms carry the runner across open space.

Combat levels add a visible gun; stomp patrols from above or press X to shoot.
