# Requirements

Store approved, uniquely identified, verifiable product and system
requirements here. Keep rationale and constraints with the requirements they
explain. Record open questions separately; an unresolved question is not
authorization to choose behavior.

Keep IDs stable and use separate IDs for different levels of requirement
detail. Current prefixes are `REQ-SYS-##` (system), `REQ-LVL-##` (level
generation), `REQ-CHR-##` (character), and `REQ-MOV-##` (movement). Record
parent/derived relationships in the requirement text or traceability matrix;
do not encode parentage into a numbered ID.
